\section*{Declarations}

\subsection*{Ethics approval and consent to participate}
Ethics approval and consent to participate were not applicable to this study because it was
a secondary computational analysis of five publicly available, de-identified benchmark
datasets; no participants were recruited and no new human data were collected.

\subsection*{Consent for publication}
Not applicable. The manuscript contains no identifiable participant information.

\subsection*{Availability of data and materials}
The five public datasets are available from their cited sources. The research
materials available at \url{https://github.com/zjj30/MedEquiSeg} include the
released model implementation, dataset-partition files with case-level text
prompts, model configurations, three-seed result tables, statistical outputs,
figures, and manuscript source. The release does not contain all orchestration
and evaluation files required for exact reconstruction of the original software
state, as described in the Limitations and Supplementary Table S11. Medical
images, masks, third-party checkpoints, and raw training logs are not
redistributed.
