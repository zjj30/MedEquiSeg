\section*{Declarations}

\subsection*{Ethics approval and consent to participate}
This study is a secondary computational analysis of five publicly released,
de-identified benchmark datasets. No participants were recruited and no new
human data were collected for this study. Ethics approval and consent to
participate were therefore not applicable. The analysis used the datasets
under the access conditions and ethical dispositions reported by their
original providers.

\subsection*{Consent for publication}
Not applicable. The manuscript contains no identifiable participant information.

\subsection*{Availability of data and materials}
The five public datasets are available from their cited sources. The audited
Public-5 Protocol V3 source-and-results package is permanently available at
\url{https://github.com/zjj30/MedEquiSeg}. It contains the protocol lock and
registry, sanitized public split manifests with their case-level text prompts,
\methodname{}/no-rewrite and baseline code, three-seed result tables and
statistical outputs, boundary and complexity source data, qualitative-audit
source data, figures, and the manuscript source. Medical images, masks,
third-party checkpoints, private-cohort artifacts, and raw training logs are
not redistributed.
