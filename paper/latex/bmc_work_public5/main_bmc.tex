\abstract{\textbf{Background:} Text can provide target and spatial context for medical image segmentation, but augmentation can invalidate spatial language and target-derived prompts can exaggerate meaningful text use. Accuracy and prompt reliability therefore require separate controls.

\textbf{Methods:} \methodname{} combines frozen pooled BioMedCLIP conditioning, two ATConv-equipped projector heads active in the evaluated forward pass, and deterministic image--mask--text augmentation. The shared policy was introduced retrospectively and entered the complete configuration together with prompt rewriting, which updates direction phrases after flips and right-angle rotations. Five public benchmarks were evaluated with three seeds, native-resolution scoring, five ordered configurations, four prompt controls, and a post hoc presence/class-stratified derangement. A separate three-seed matched BUSI rerun held image--mask augmentation plans fixed and varied only rewriting.

\textbf{Results:} \methodname{} achieved a five-dataset macro-average Dice of $87.87\%$ and IoU of $80.97\%$. Versus the horizontal-flip Base reference, Dice was higher by $1.46$ points (95\% dataset-stratified bootstrap interval $0.95$ to $1.99$) and IoU by $1.74$ points (95\% interval $1.13$ to $2.34$). The largest adjacent Dice difference was $+0.84$ points between BioMedCLIP plus ATConv and the complete configuration; this transition jointly added shared augmentation and rewriting and did not identify either standalone effect. In the matched BUSI rerun, rewriting minus no rewriting was $+1.17$ Dice points (95\% hierarchical bootstrap interval $-1.58$ to $+4.17$). True text exceeded presence/class-stratified derangement by $+5.63$ Dice points (95\% interval $+4.71$ to $+6.67$), versus $+14.66$ under unstratified shuffling.

\textbf{Conclusions:} The complete configuration was higher than Base in the ordered analysis, but the sequence does not isolate standalone causal effects. The BUSI rewriting interval included zero, providing no clear accuracy advantage in that dataset and no decomposition of the five-dataset transition. The shuffled-prompt gap was attenuated under one fixed presence/class-stratified assignment, a pattern consistent with coarse class/presence confounding; its residual remains conditional on target-derived prompts and that assignment. These results characterize benchmark performance and prompt sensitivity, not clinical-report understanding or deployment generalization. Code and supporting materials are available at \url{https://github.com/zjj30/MedEquiSeg}.}
